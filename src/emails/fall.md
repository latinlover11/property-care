Fall is here in the Colorado front range, and that means it's time to get your property ready before the first hard freeze and the snow that follows. A little prep now saves a lot of expensive repairs in the spring.

Here are our top fall property-care tips:

**1. Clear the gutters.** Leaves and pine needles clog gutters fast. Blocked gutters lead to ice dams and water damage to fascia and foundations. We'll get them flowing freely.

**2. Winterize sprinklers.** A blown-out irrigation system is the difference between a $120 fix and a $2,000 pipe-replacement job. We handle blowouts for every system type.

**3. Leaf cleanup & final mow.** Dropped leaves smother lawns and breed mold. A final cleanup keeps your yard healthy through dormancy and looking sharp for the holidays.

**4. Check fences, decks & seals.** Fall is the best time to stain a fence or reseal a deck before winter moisture sets in. We also re-caulk windows and doors to cut heat loss.

**5. Book snow prep.** Plows, shoveling, and ice-melt plans fill up fast once the first storm hits. Get on the schedule now.
